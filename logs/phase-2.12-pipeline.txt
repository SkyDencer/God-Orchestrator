## Key fix discovered during development
The vitest runner on Windows outputs ANSI color codes (e.g. [32m✓[39m) which
broke the regex-based parsers in TestVerifier.parseVitest() / parseSummaryLines().
Root-cause: process.stdout capture via ProcessManager preserved the ANSI sequences
from vitest's TTY detection. Fix: strip ANSI escape sequences in TestVerifier.verify()
before passing output to the parser.
See: src/verification/test-verifier.ts line 71-73
